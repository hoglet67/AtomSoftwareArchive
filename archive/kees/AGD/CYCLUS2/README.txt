Cyclus Part1:
-------------

Cyclus 2 - Side B

Cyclus continues to wander, but is still far from home. Will you help Cyclus? On each level, you must unlock the exit to the next level by taking all the coins around the screen and then guide him there.
This second load has different mechanics.
You control Cyclus, and although he will move independently, you can make him change direction and even jump between the walls of the scene. If you press the fire key, Cyclus will accelerate in the direction he is facing. Keep in mind that Cyclus will always be attached to the floor or wall through which it is moving.

For example: 
- If Cyclus is moving horizontally to the right, if you press the "left" key, the snail will change its direction and move to the left and vice versa.
- If Cyclus is moving vertically upwards and you press the “down” button, the snail will change its direction and move downwards (and vice versa).
- If Cyclus is moving horizontally and you press the "up" button, Cyclus will jump vertically until it lands on the wall or ceiling above it.
- If Cyclus is moving horizontally but "upside down" and you press the "down" button, Cyclus will jump vertically down to land on the ground below him.
- The same will happen when you move vertically and press right or left.

Watch out for the enemies that appear and keep in mind that each screen has a limited time to be completed.
You must avoid at all costs touching the spikes that are scattered on the screen. Also avoid touching the green radioactive liquid or you will lose a life. The same will happen if you touch the lava that appears on some screens.
In some levels the ground will be frozen, be careful when stepping on some areas as Cyclus will slip and move at a high speed.

- In the panel below you can see the level you are in, the time left, the number of coins you have left to collect and the number of lives you have.
- If you load the game in 128K you will be able to enjoy ingame music. If you load it in 48K, the game will have beeper sounds only.

Design, programming & loading screen: Miguel Ángel Tejedor López (aka. Miguetelo)

Controls:
The controls are redefinable, although by default, if you choose "keyboard" the keys will be…
  O   - Left
  P   - Right
  Q   - Up
  A   - Down
SPACE - Accelerate
  H   - Pause
  G   - Abort game

You can choose Sinclair or Kempston Joystick if you wish.

===================================================================
System requirements:
===================================================================

- Standard Acorn Atom
- 32 KB RAM
- 8 KB video RAM (#8000-#9FFF)
- Joystick connected to keyboard matrix (Optional)
- Joystick connected to PORTB AtoMMC interface (Optional)

===================================================================
Joystick (optional JOYMMC):
===================================================================

The joystick is connected to PORTB of the AtoMMC interface with 
softwareversion 2.9. The connections are like this:

AtoMMC  Joystick
-----------------
 PB0  -  Right
 PB1  -  Left
 PB2  -  Down
 PB3  -  Up
 PB4  -  Jump
 PB5  -  nc
 PB6  -  nc
 PB7  -  nc

 GND  -  GND

If direction is active, bit = 1

===================================================================
Joystick (optional JOYKEY):
===================================================================

The joystick is connected parallel to row 1 of the keyboard matrix.

  O   - Left
  P   - Right
  Q   - Up
  A   - Down
SPACE - Fire
  H   - Pause
  G   - Retry level

===================================================================
Tape/Disk and AtoMMC version:
===================================================================

Tape version:

  CYCLUS2.CSW, Tapefile for Atomulator, to start the game, type: *RUN"AGD"

Disk version:

  CYCLUS2.DSK, Diskfile for emulators, to start the game, type *RUN"CY2RUN"

AtoMMC version:

  CY2RUN  = Basic introscreen
  CY2SCR  = Titlescreen
  CY2CODE = Gamecode

  To start the game, type: *CY2RUN
