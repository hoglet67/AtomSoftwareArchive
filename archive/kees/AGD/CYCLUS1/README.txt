Cyclus Part1:
-------------

Cyclus 1 - Side A

You are Cyclus, a humble snail who after being captured for culinary purposes decides to escape his sad fate. You must help Cyclus returns home avoiding the innumerable obstacles that await him.
Cyclus will appear through a pipe and you must guide him to reach the other pipe that will allow him to advance to the next level and which will have a sign that says “Goal”.

- Avoid the spikes and the enemies: spiders, snakes and bulldozers.
- Cyclus moves independently, so you must guide him with the boxes on which he can walk or bounce to change his direction.
- You will control a crosshair that you can move around the screen and by pressing fire you will place a box (if you press fire on a box that is already on the screen, you will be able to eliminate it). The pointer will not be able to pass through walls.
- Each screen will have a limited number of boxes that can be placed.
- The boxes have a countdown. Once the counter reaches zero, the box will disappear and return to your inventory. (If there are no boxes in your inventory, you will not be able to place any).
- You can also bounce enemies and other characters that appear on the boxes. They will also be able to walk on them just like you.
- In some screens a key will appear. Getting it, you can open closed doors that block the exit and procced. Be careful and think about when to collect them, since the doors will disappear instantly and it may not be the most convenient. In some screens you must use certain strategy when collecting and using the keys.
- You will see mobile arrows that point upwards in some levels, in those places Cyclus will be able to climb the walls. (You can put a box over those arrows to avoid climb if you don't want that to).
- You can’t cross the white walls but the bulldozers can do it (your cross hair will not be able to cross them either). Guide the bulldozers to destroy these walls, but be careful not to touch them, if you do, you will die.
- The number of boxes and lives remaining, in addition to the level you are at, appears in the right panel.
- This first load of the game consists of 35 levels.
- If you load the game in 128K you will be able to enjoy ingame music. If you load it in 48K, the game will have beeper sounds only.

TIPS: 
- Pause the game with the "pause" key whenever you want to be able to plan a level.
- You can use boxes to block enemies.
- In some of the screens where bulldozers appear, you will have to guide them and eliminate the walls. Be careful or you could get trapped and have to retry the level.
- You can also use the boxes to block one of the snails on more advanced levels.
- In this first load there is no time, so take your´s.

Controls:
The controls are redefinable, although by default, if you choose "keyboard" the keys will be…
  O   - Left
  P   - Right
  Q   - Up
  A   - Down
SPACE - Fire
  H   - Pause
  G   - Retry level

You can choose Sinclair or Kempston Joystick if you wish.

===================================================================
System requirements:
===================================================================

- Standard Acorn Atom
- 32 KB RAM
- 8 KB video RAM (#8000-#9FFF)
- Joystick connected to keyboard matrix (Optional)
- Joystick connected to PORTB AtoMMC interface (Optional)

===================================================================
Joystick (optional JOYMMC):
===================================================================

The joystick is connected to PORTB of the AtoMMC interface with 
softwareversion 2.9. The connections are like this:

AtoMMC  Joystick
-----------------
 PB0  -  Right
 PB1  -  Left
 PB2  -  Down
 PB3  -  Up
 PB4  -  Jump
 PB5  -  nc
 PB6  -  nc
 PB7  -  nc

 GND  -  GND

If direction is active, bit = 1

===================================================================
Joystick (optional JOYKEY):
===================================================================

The joystick is connected parallel to row 1 of the keyboard matrix.

  O   - Left
  P   - Right
  Q   - Up
  A   - Down
SPACE - Fire
  H   - Pause
  G   - Retry level

===================================================================
Tape/Disk and AtoMMC version:
===================================================================

Tape version:

  CYCLUS1.CSW, Tapefile for Atomulator, to start the game, type: *RUN"AGD"

Disk version:

  CYCLUS1.DSK, Diskfile for emulators, to start the game, type *RUN"CY1RUN"

AtoMMC version:

  CY1RUN  = Basic introscreen
  CY1SCR  = Titlescreen
  CY1PAN  = Panel
  CY1CODE = Gamecode

  To start the game, type: *CY1RUN
