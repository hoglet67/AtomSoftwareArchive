The Hairy Fly:
--------------

The Story

For generations there have been many failed attempts to recover the magical Chalice that was stolen from your once happy and prosperous world. 
Now guarded by an ancient alien race, it’s up to you to take back what is yours, restoring peace and harmony once again!
As the hero in Mr Hair’s latest adventure you must bravely journey through this treacherous world, searching for the stolen energy cells that provide power to the Kingdoms key chambers to  release the Chalice.
Not everything is at it appears in this alien landscape, you must be cunning, you must be determined and you must even be willing to transform yourself into THE FLY to be able to reach the unreachable. 
You are the last hope to bring back the magical chalice and save your world before it’s too late.

CONTROLS

KEYBOARD 
O - LEFT   
P - RIGHT 
Q - UP      
A - DOWN 
SPACE - JUMP and QUICK DROP

===================================================================
System requirements:
===================================================================

- Standard Acorn Atom
- 32 KB RAM
- 8 KB video RAM (#8000-#9FFF)
- Joystick connected to keyboard matrix (Optional)
- Joystick connected to PORTB AtoMMC interface (Optional)

===================================================================
Joystick (optional JOYMMC):
===================================================================

The joystick is connected to PORTB of the AtoMMC interface with 
softwareversion 2.9. The connections are like this:

AtoMMC  Joystick
-----------------
 PB0  -  Right
 PB1  -  Left
 PB2  -  Down
 PB3  -  Up
 PB4  -  Jump
 PB5  -  nc
 PB6  -  nc
 PB7  -  nc

 GND  -  GND

If direction is active, bit = 1

===================================================================
Joystick (optional JOYKEY):
===================================================================

The joystick is connected parallel to row 1 of the keyboard matrix.

    nokey - fire
	G - Right
	3 - Left
	- - Down
	Q - Up

===================================================================
Tape/Disk and AtoMMC version:
===================================================================

Tape version:

  HAIRFLY.CSW, Tapefile for Atomulator, to start the game, type: *RUN"AGD"

Disk version:

  HAIRFLY.DSK, Diskfile for emulators, to start the game, type *RUN"HFRUN"

AtoMMC version:

  HFRUN  = Basic introscreen
  HFSCR  = Titlescreen
  HFCODE = Gamecode

  To start the game, type: *RUN"HFRUN"
