Dark Knight 2:
--------------

STORY

A criminal gang has wreaked havoc in the city by kidnapping a group of scientists, the only ones capable of finding an antidote to the virus that they have spread throughout the city. Night falls, it's time to do justice...

THE GAME

The game is divided into 4 different levels. 
There are closed doors supervised by terminals, destroy them to be able to open them or look for an alternative. (The doors will close again after a while.)
You have several special moves: weapon throw, double jump, wall grab, and the ability to duck to dodge your enemies' bullets.
Some hosts change positions on the map each game.
A cold beer gives you life.

Level 1
Free the 6 scientists kidnapped by the terrorists.

Level 2
Drive around avoiding traffic and collecting 10 bombs scattered around the city.

Level 3
Find a sample of the virus and give it to scientists to make an antidote.

Level 4
Storm the terrorist base and finish off its leader.

===================================================================
System requirements:
===================================================================

- Standard Acorn Atom
- 32 KB RAM
- 8 KB video RAM (#8000-#9FFF)
- Joystick connected to keyboard matrix (Optional)
- Joystick connected to PORTB AtoMMC interface (Optional)

===================================================================
Joystick (optional JOYMMC):
===================================================================

The joystick is connected to PORTB of the AtoMMC interface with 
softwareversion 2.9. The connections are like this:

AtoMMC  Joystick
-----------------
 PB0  -  Right
 PB1  -  Left
 PB2  -  Down
 PB3  -  Up
 PB4  -  Jump
 PB5  -  nc
 PB6  -  nc
 PB7  -  nc

 GND  -  GND

If direction is active, bit = 1

===================================================================
Joystick (optional JOYKEY):
===================================================================

The joystick is connected parallel to row 1 of the keyboard matrix.

  Q   - UP
  A   - DOWN  
  O   - LEFT
  P   - RIGHT
  K   - FIRE 

===================================================================
Tape/Disk and AtoMMC version:
===================================================================

Tape version:

  DARKKNIGHT2.CSW, Tapefile for Atomulator, to start the game, type: *RUN"AGD"

Disk version:

  DARKKNIGHT2.DSK, Diskfile for emulators, to start the game, type *RUN"DN2RUN"

AtoMMC version:

  DN2RUN  = Basic introscreen
  DN2SCR  = Titlescreen
  DN2SCR2 = Titlescreen2
  DN2PAN  = Panel
  DN2CODE = Gamecode

  To start the game, type: *DN2RUN
