Ziona Quest:
------------

PLOT:
-----
The Year is 1023, the call went out for brave souls to come join the quest to help humanity free themselves and return the Earth to her true heavenly glory...
...you answered.

WELCOME BRAVE SOUL!!     
                       
Ziona Quest is a frantic platformer where skill, strategy and quick reactions are essential if you are to complete your mission.

FEATURES:
---------

15 Blistering levels of increasing challenge
Detailed colourful graphics and animation
Finely tuned and balanced gameplay
Every game unique
Quality soundtrack by NQ
The chance to save humanity!

Ziona Quest runs on any device with a ZX Spectrum emulator or on a real ZX Spectrum 128K computer.
We recommend the above methods for optimal play but you can also play right now in your browser
(for browser sound control: Press F9 then click 'Mute' on the left)

The game comes in two parts;
ZIONA QUEST : TRAINING - A short training intro to get you used to the game before embarking on your mission. It is recommended you complete this first!
ZIONA QUEST : GAME - The full game.

EACH LEVEL HAS THREE PHASES:
PHASE 1: WIN TRUTH BOMB
PHASE 2: GUIDE HUMANS TO TEMPLE
PHASE 3: ZAP NEEDLERS / RESCUE BROTHER

PHASE 1: WIN TRUTH BOMB

Collect a ROCK, throw at TV, collect TV 'CORE' and take to the TEMPLE.
Take 3x 'CORES' to the TEMPLE and enter to win your 'TRUTH BOMB'.

PHASE 2: GUIDE HUMANS TO THE TEMPLE

Drop 'TRUTH BOMBS'  in HUMANS path. They will react by freaking out or getting angry. Shoot FREAKERS / FIGHTERS with your arrows to WAKEN them (turn blue) then guide them to the TEMPLE.
Shoot WAKENED HUMANS to change direction, then push them to a ladder - shoot again to make them go up.

PHASE 3: ZAP REMAINING NEEDLERS AND RESCUE BROTHER
NEEDLERS are despatched by a number of ARROW hits or by a 'GOLDEN ROCK'.
During PHASE 3 your BROTHER escapes the MK BEAM and starts wandering around. Hit BROTHER with arrows until he turns GOLD, then touch him to send him skyward - he will give you an EXTRA LIFE and a 'GOLDEN ROCK' which can take out a NEEDLER with one hit!

The TEMPLE LIGHT will FLASH when you can enter and reach the NEXT LEVEL.
THE SUN represents your time limit for each level.
When THE SUN reaches the peak of the TEMPLE time is up.

Keep moving brave one!

CONTROLS:
---------
The game is controlled using your KEYBOARD:

  Q   - UP 
  A   - DOWN
  O   - LEFT
  P   - RIGHT
SPACE - FIRE

PAUSE GAME - 'H' - press any key to resume.
MUSIC OFF  - 'M' - current tune will stop.

PLAYER MOVES:
-------------

WALK LEFT/RIGHT - press  LEFT or RIGHT
JUMP - press UP
DUCK - press DOWN
ROLL - hold DOWN and press LEFT or RIGHT
FIRE ARROW/THROW ROCK - press FIRE
DROP 'TRUTH BOMB' - press DOWN and hold FIRE
PICK UP 'TRUTH BOMB' - press UP

EXTRA INFO / TIPS:
------------------

ENERGY:
-------

Your ENERGY BAR shows how much ENERGY you currently have.
A FULL-SPECTRUM ENERGY BAR indicates that you have all of your abilities (player is WHITE).
If your FULL-SPECTRUM ENERGY BAR hits zero you will TURN RED, slow down, loose your current weapon and be unable to jump.
A GOLD ENERGY BAR indicates that your abilities are restricted (PLAYER is RED).
WARNING! If you are hit by a shot when you are RED you will die!
To recharge (when player is RED) meditate - to meditate simply do nothing!
When recharged you will regain your FULL-SPETRUM ENERGY BAR and your full abilities will return (player is WHITE).

ENERGY LOSS:
Coming into contact with UNCONSCIOUS HUMANS will drain your energy.
Getting shot by NEEDLER or SMARTGUN will wipeout all of your FULL-SPECTRUM ENERGY instantly.

ENERGY RECHARGE:
You can RECHARGE your FULL-SPECTRUM ENERGY by touching a disabled NEEDLER.
You can RECHARGE from an energy wipeout by meditating.

WEAPONS:
--------

ROCKS:
Collect a ROCK from the pile on top of the LEVEL COUNTER by pressing 'DOWN'. 
You can only carry one ROCK at a time. Throw wisely!         
ROCKS will have the following effect on:
- NEEDLERS : disabled temporarily
- HUMANS : changes direction.
- FIGHTERS : changes back to Sleeper                           
- TV : switches off and releases one 'CORE'. A third hit smashes TV.
- SMARTGUN : A well timed hit will disable the SMARTGUN temporarily until your BROTHER is rescued or you reach the NEXT LEVEL.

ARROWS:
You can collect ARROWS from your brother (bottom left of screen) who is trapped under the 'MK BEAM'.
Be careful though as touching him when he is under the beam will drain your energy.
You can fire unlimited ARROWS but will lose them if your energy hits zero or you pick up a ROCK.

ARROWS will have the following affect on:
- HUMANS: Change direction
- FIGHTERS: AWAKEN
- FREAKERS: AWAKEN
- NEEDLERS: Disabled for a short period of time.
- BROTHER: Raises his vibration and colour until he becomes GOLD

TRUTH BOMBS:
You will gain your 'TRUTH BOMBS' if you enter the TEMPLE after delivering three TV CORES.
Drop 'TRUTH BOMBS' by pressing 'DOWN' and holding 'FIRE'.
You have unlimited 'TRUTH BOMBS 'but can only drop ONE at a time. 
Pick up dropped 'TRUTH BOMBS' by pressing 'UP'.
NEEDLERS clear any 'TRUTH BOMBS' they encounter.
When a HUMAN or NEEDLER hits a 'TRUTH BOMB'  you are free to make another drop.

GOLDEN ROCK:
You will win the GOLDEN ROCK if you rescue your BROTHER. 
The GOLDEN ROCK will take out NEEDLERS with ONE HIT!
You only get ONE SHOT so throw well!

CHARACTERS:
-----------

HUMANS (UNCONSCIOUS) - COLOUR: WHITE

The humans are under a spell and wander back and forth staring at their phones oblivious to what is going on around them.
When humans are hit by a TRUTH BOMB they will either become angry and go on the attack or freak out and float around aimlessly.
An ARROW hit will make them change direction.

FIGHTERS - COLOUR: MAGENTA
Angry humans will run around in a dissonant rage. If they get you, you die!
Shoot with ARROW to calm down and awaken.

FREAKERS - COLOUR GREEN / BLUE
Cause no harm and will float around until shot with an ARROW.

WAKENED HUMANS - COLOUR: BLUE
Newly WAKENED HUMANS wait to be guided to the TEMPLE.
Hit with ARROWS to turn them around then push in desired direction.
When WAKENED reach a LADDER they will wait there until hit with ARROW.
WAKENED are SAFE from shots and NEEDLERS when standing under a LADDER.
If WAKENED are touched by NEEDLER they will be put back to sleep.
If WAKENED are shot by NEEDLER or SMARTGUN they will revert back to either FIGHTER or FREAKER.
WAKENED HUMANS will duck when you duck. Handy for dodging NEEDLERS and SHOTS!

NEEDLER - COLOUR: RED
These A.I. controlled Dr.Bots are heartless, soulless and merciless.
The NEEDLER's mission is to keep the HUMANS unconscious.
WAKENED HUMANS will be 'injected' and put back to sleep if caught by NEEDLER.
PLAYER will be killed if hit by NEEDLER
NEEDLERS will remove any 'TRUTH BOMBS' they find.

BROTHER - COLOUR: DEEP BLUE > GOLD
Your BROTHER has been trapped under the MK BEAM but does his best to help you by offering his ARROWS.
When the MK BEAM is cut (when all humans are liberated) your BROTHER will break loose and wander around the level.
Your BROTHER has a DEEP BLUE colour when he starts wandering. Shoot him with your ARROWS until his colour becomes GOLD. Touch your BROTHER when he is GOLD and he will ascend giving you an EXTRA LIFE and a special GOLDEN ROCK.

SMARTGUN
The SMARTGUN is active from LEVEL 4 onwards and fires a shot every so often.
The SMARTGUN will switch off during the PHASE 3 but will re-activate when your BROTHER is rescued.

===================================================================
System requirements:
===================================================================

- Standard Acorn Atom
- 32 KB RAM
- 8 KB video RAM (#8000-#9FFF)
- Joystick connected to keyboard matrix (Optional)
- Joystick connected to PORTB AtoMMC interface (Optional)

===================================================================
Joystick (optional JOYMMC):
===================================================================

The joystick is connected to PORTB of the AtoMMC interface with 
softwareversion 2.9. The connections are like this:

AtoMMC  Joystick
-----------------
 PB0  -  Right
 PB1  -  Left
 PB2  -  Down
 PB3  -  Up
 PB4  -  Jump
 PB5  -  nc
 PB6  -  nc
 PB7  -  nc

 GND  -  GND

If direction is active, bit = 1

===================================================================
Joystick (optional JOYKEY):
===================================================================

The joystick is connected parallel to row 1 of the keyboard matrix.

  Q   - UP
  A   - DOWN  
  O   - LEFT
  P   - RIGHT
SPACE - FIRE

  M   - Switch between SNOWBALLS and FLAMETHROWER
  H   - PAUSE GAME

===================================================================
Tape/Disk and AtoMMC version:
===================================================================

Tape version:

  ZIONA.CSW, Tapefile for Atomulator, to start the game, type: *RUN"AGD"

Disk version:

AtoMMC version:

  ZIRUN  = Basic introscreen
  ZISCR  = Titlescreen
  ZICODE = Gamecode

  To start the game, type: *ZIRUN
