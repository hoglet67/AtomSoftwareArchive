Enter the Prilel:
-----------------


Your adventurer must enter an empty, desolated city, where it is said that 
an artificial intelligence, known as the PRILEL, has taken physical shape 
and is feeding off its inhabitants dreams and hopes…

Good luck!

----------------------------------------------------------------------------
LOADING INSTRUCTIONS 
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Connect the EAR socket on your Spectrum to the EAR socket 
   on your recorder and ensure the MIC lead is disconnected.
2. Place the cassette tape in the recorder and rewind to the beginning. 
3. Type either LOAD "PRILEL" or LOAD "" 
4. Press PLAY on the cassette recorder. 
5. ENTER THE PRILEL will now load automatically and a message 
   will appear on screen after several seconds. If loading is 
   unsuccessful, rewind the cassette, adjust the VOLUME control 
   on the recorder and try again. 
6. Select control type
7. Press Ø to start game

----------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLLING YOUR ADVENTURER
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

KEYBOARD CONTROLS 
LEFT  Your adventurer will move left using
      the O key. 
RIGHT Your adventurer will move right using
      the P key. 
UP    Your adventurer will move up and
      jump using the Q key. 
DOWN  Your adventurer will move down using
      the A key. 
FIRE  Your adventurer will use his gun 
      when the M key is pressed. 

JOYSTICK CONTROLS 
Your Adventurer can be fully controlled by using the KEMPSTON INTERFACE  or the SINCLAIR INTERFACE II joystick.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SOUND ADVICE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

For better sound effects you can increase the volume by connecting 
the MIC socket on your Spectrum to the MIC on your recorder. 
Disconnect the EAR lead, remove the cassette tape from the recorder and 
press the PLAY button, the sound may be amplified through the 
loudspeaker of the recorder.

===================================================================
System requirements:
===================================================================

- Standard Acorn Atom
- 32 KB RAM
- 8 KB video RAM (#8000-#9FFF)
- Joystick connected to keyboard matrix (Optional)
- Joystick connected to PORTB AtoMMC interface (Optional)

===================================================================
Joystick (optional JOYMMC):
===================================================================

The joystick is connected to PORTB of the AtoMMC interface with 
softwareversion 2.9. The connections are like this:

AtoMMC  Joystick
-----------------
 PB0  -  Right
 PB1  -  Left
 PB2  -  Down
 PB3  -  Up
 PB4  -  Jump
 PB5  -  nc
 PB6  -  nc
 PB7  -  nc

 GND  -  GND

If direction is active, bit = 1

===================================================================
Joystick (optional JOYKEY):
===================================================================

The joystick is connected parallel to row 1 of the keyboard matrix.

  Q   - UP
  O   - LEFT
  P   - RIGHT
SPACE - FIRE

===================================================================
Tape/Disk and AtoMMC version:
===================================================================

Tape version:

  PRILEL.CSW, Tapefile for Atomulator, to start the game, type: *RUN"AGD"

Disk version:

  PRILEL.DSK, Diskfile for emulators, to start the game, type *RUN"EPRUN"

AtoMMC version:

  EPRUN  = Basic introscreen
  EPSCR  = Titlescreen
  EPCODE = Gamecode

  To start the game, type: *EPRUN
